Selecting a Restoration Professional in Orlando, FL

Home / Blog / Choosing a Water Damage Restoration Company in Orlando, FL

Picking a restoration company is a major decision for any Orlando, FL owner dealing with water damage. Your out-of-pocket cost, the pace of the work, the thoroughness of the repair, and the odds of secondary trouble such as mold months later all depend on who you hire. Unfortunately, the restoration industry has its share of unqualified operators who may leave you with incomplete work, inflated bills, or worse. Here is what to look for when sorting the best restoration firms from everyone else.

Confirm the Crew Is IICRC Certified

In the restoration industry, the leading credential comes from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC). IICRC-certified technicians have completed rigorous training in water damage restoration science, including proper extraction techniques, structural drying methodology, moisture measurement, and mold prevention. Look for two current IICRC certifications on the crew: WRT (Water Damage Restoration) and ASD (Applied Structural Drying). Steer clear of any firm unable to show proof of them.

Ask for Proof of Insurance and Licenses

Any company you let work in your home needs full insurance, meaning general liability as well as workers' compensation. Request certificates of insurance and verify they're current. A company lacking coverage can leave you on the hook when someone gets hurt or more property is damaged mid-project. Also verify that the company holds any business licenses required by Orlando or Florida regulations.

Ask How Fast They Respond

With water damage, the clock is always running. Costs and damage climb with every hour that passes. Real 24/7 emergency service, with crews that reach your neighborhood fast, should be part of the deal. Find out their typical response time for Orlando, and verify the emergency number goes to a real person, since an answering service or voicemail only adds delay.

Look at Their Equipment and Drying Process

Professional restoration requires professional equipment. Three questions cover it: do they use thermal imaging to detect moisture, are their dehumidifiers LGR units (the industry standard), and is extraction done with truck-mounted equipment, which outperforms portable-only operations? Daily moisture readings, with equipment adjusted to match them, are the mark of a reputable firm, so question any company that simply drops off machines and hopes.

Read Reviews and Call References

You can learn a lot about past performance from what customers post online. Check Google, Yelp, and the BBB for consistent patterns of quality, professionalism, and customer satisfaction. The most useful reviews talk about communication, punctuality, help with insurance, and how the finished restoration turned out. Don't hesitate to ask for references from recent water damage projects in Orlando.

Know How They Price and Bill Insurance

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Xactimate and similar industry-standard pricing programs, recognized and accepted by insurance carriers, are used by many quality contractors. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. Be wary of any company that wants a large payment up front or will not work with insurance.
