How to Tell You Need Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Flooring flexes more than it should in the spot that was flooded.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • The lower part of cabinet sides or bases feels soft, looks swollen or is peeling.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.

How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • Take pictures of damaged materials prior to removal, since the insurer may want them later.

What a Professional Brings to Water Damage Repair

Rebuilding after a water loss differs from a normal remodel. A rebuilder must know the path the water took, the harm it caused and if the structure hidden by the wall is actually dry. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. Knowing the background stops issues from being hidden behind new wallboard and a coat of paint.

The second skill is matching. You will see a visible patch every single day. Wall texture is blended, paint is feathered out to natural breaks, and flooring is sourced to line up with the boards already there when available. When a match is not possible, we lay out options, such as replacing flooring to a doorway. We also handle permits where the work requires them and keep your insurer updated.
